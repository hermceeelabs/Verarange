What to Wear to Your Couples & Engagement Session

Styling Direction by Miley • Vera Range Photography

1. Coordinate Rather Than Match Exactly

The best galleries feature complementary tones rather than identical shirts. Choose two to three foundational colors that harmonize with open pastures and timber lines.

Rich burgundies, deep espresso, vintage olive, warm sand, and creamy linen complement each other beautifully while letting both of your individual personalities shine through.

2. Embrace Textures Over Bold Prints

Heavy busy patterns can distract from authentic facial expressions. Instead, elevate your wardrobe through organic texture.

Washed denim, ribbed knit sweaters, suede fringes, weathered leather belts, and flowy linen dresses capture natural movement when the wind picks up across the field.

3. Footwear Built For Adventure

Great sessions involve walking through grass, exploring pasture fences, and dancing under the sunset. Wear boots that feel broken in and comfortable.

Classic leather riding boots, tooled ropers, and ankle boots add that iconic timeless touch while allowing you to move naturally and focus on each other.
