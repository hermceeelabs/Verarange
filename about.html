The Heart Behind Vera Range

Meet Miley

Based in Southwest Missouri • Available for Travel Worldwide

Hi there! I am the vision and founder behind Vera Range Photography. What started out as a deep love for honest moments and wide open pastures has grown into a luxury Western photography brand for lovers, adventurers, and families who appreciate authentic beauty.

I believe in photographs that carry emotion you can feel fifty years from now. I don’t believe in stiff posing or turning your wedding into an endless photoshoot. Instead, I step back to capture the quiet glances, the dirty hems from dancing in the grass, the laughter between courses, and the deep reverence of your vows.

When I’m not behind the lens traveling for destination weddings, you can find me exploring the Missouri countryside, spending time with family, or planning the next big road trip.
